Who we are

Ndika Engineers Limited is a Tanzanian consultancy founded in 2014 that specialises in water resources management, hydrology and hydrological modelling, flood monitoring, environmental flows, GIS and remote sensing, and catchment management.

Our team combines senior academics from the University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM) and Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA) with practising engineers, giving clients rigorous science together with practical, field-based delivery.

Ndika has a long working partnership with HYDROC GmbH, with joint assignments for NELSAP (Akagera River avulsion), GIZ/Nile Basin Initiative (environmental flows guidance), the Kenya Ministry of Water and Irrigation (Nzoia watershed), KfW (Katavi and Mahale National Parks) and the Songwe River Basin flood early warning system.
